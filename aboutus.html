About Us

Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Sustainable Cotton Trade

Advaita Exports is an India-based trading company operating across international markets with a singular focus: to build a cleaner, more reliable cotton supply chain. We specialize in the sourcing and export of:

  • Organic Cotton
  • Organic Yarn
  • BCI Cotton
  • Extra-Long Staple (ELS) Cotton

With a commitment to quality, consistency, and responsible sourcing, we serve as the link between ethical growers and global textile manufacturers.

Our Journey

What began as a small venture rooted in cotton-growing regions of India has now evolved into a recognized name in both domestic and global trade. Built on relationships and reputation, Advaita Exports has grown by aligning with partners who share our vision — to deliver premium cotton, responsibly sourced and transparently traded. Wireframe 6Today, we work with certified growers, spinning mills, and textile houses across multiple continents, while staying grounded in our founding values: Integrity, Sustainability, and Long-Term Thinking.

Our Leadership

Led by a team of professionals with deep experience in agri-trading and cotton supply chains, our leadership blends operational efficiency with a sharp understanding of market trends. We are also proud to work closely with farmer cooperatives and compliance bodies to ensure every step we take supports the larger vision of sustainable global trade.

From India to the World

Headquartered in India, Advaita Exports operates across Asia, Europe, and the Americas through a growing network of clients and sourcing partners. Our market knowledge and on-ground presence enable us to offer customized solutions across geographies while maintaining full visibility and control over the sourcing process.
